**Other ways to cut the waiting (not built; yes or no each):**

1. **Talk while walking:** more lines said as captions while the child holds to walk, instead of stopping: Wiseman walking a few steps with Christian before the hill; Obstinate and Pliable's first lines as they catch up.
2. **Quicker hand-offs:** a line moves on by itself sooner after it ends (now about 1.25 s), and a line already heard once can be tapped away at once.
3. **The old English rule:** at most one old word or turn of phrase per line, kept where it is famous or flavourful ("Flee from the wrath to come", "Life! Life! Eternal life!", "Woe is me, for I am undone!", "Knock, and it shall be opened unto you", "God-speed", "Tush!", "How now, good fellow!"); the rest plain. The rewordings below follow it.
4. **One budget for every level:** about 2 minutes of speech, never more than about 5 lines in a row before the child does something.
