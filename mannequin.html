The mannequin: a skeleton that walks, and clothes that follow it

Left: the bare mannequin and its bones (green: the shoulder line and the hip line, which turn against each other as he walks). Middle: Christian's clothes built on it, a dark quiet silhouette. Right: his old picture frames, only to compare the feel. Below: walking at the game's size.

His measures: move the sliders to the ones you like

Open a part, move its sliders; the close-up shows that part and stays in view. The hood, and the bundle on top of his burden, can also be shaped by hand: press "Shape it by hand" under their sliders and drag the points of the edge. When you like him, send me the lines under the sliders (or the link).

Drag a point: the edge runs through the points. You can start your finger just beside a point.
