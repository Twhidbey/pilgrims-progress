# Pilgrim's Progress: notes for Claude

A gentle side-scrolling game for kids ages 7 to 10, following Bunyan's *The Pilgrim's Progress*. No build step, no dependencies. **`HANDOFF.md` starts with "START HERE": read that first in a new chat.**

**The book is in the repo (build 46):** `book/pilgrims-progress-part-one.txt` is Bunyan's own text of Part One (public domain; no later editors' introductions), with section numbers like `{18}` for reference. Level 1 covers `{10}` to about `{28}`; the Slough of Despond starts at `{29}`. Check the book before writing or changing story lines.

**STAY CLOSE TO THE STORY (owner, build 48: "can you put sticking close to story in the notes").** The game follows Bunyan's order of events and his words where a child can follow them. Before adding, moving or rewording a story moment, read it in the book file and keep to it; where the game departs from the book, say so to the owner and list it here. Known departures in Level 1: if the child lets go at his own door he stops and answers his family (the owner's framed picture, build 45; in the book he never stops or answers; holding on runs him straight past, as in the book); the city gate and wall at the end (not in the book); the book's first scene at home, before the fields, is left out.

## Art direction (owner, Nov 14 2026)

- **Scenery is drawn in code** (canvas / vector) for full control. **People are monochrome watercolour figures rotoscoped from AI videos** (since builds 15 to 17; earlier: black silhouette sprites).
- **Atmospheric layered silhouettes, not video-game looking.** Each depth layer is basically ONE colour. Layers overlap with transparency and haze so together they read like a painted scene. **Pale far layers, darker near layers.**
- **Soft rain on the left clearing to warm golden light on the right.**
- Simple, natural, calm. **Not cartoonish.**
- The look was settled in `style-lab.html` first, then moved into the game (build 6). Try new looks in the lab before changing the game.
- **Christian is a monochrome watercolour** (owner, build 15: "I'm cool leaving him as watercolor, which you can make more monochromatic"). His walk is **rotoscoped from an AI video** (Veo, started from the original watercolour Christian): 38 frames of one full walk cycle, made grey (the painting's light and dark only) and tinted in the game for the weather. Earlier tries, all rejected as jumpy or strange: AI pose sheets (never a true walk cycle, measured), blending poses, AI body on code-drawn legs (the old `walk-lab.html`, deleted in build 26). **Lesson: AI image tools can't make walk cycles; AI video can.** Owner on the result: "that walk looks AMAZING, more like this for other characters". Since build 17 **everyone** is a video character: Christian (walk, stop, stand breathing), Pliable (walk), Obstinate (walk), Evangelist (stand, raise arm and point).
- **Clothes must be timeless** (owner, Nov 14 2026: a post-apocalyptic version may come later): worn, handmade, layered cloth (hoods, cloaks, wraps, sashes, satchels, staffs) that suits the 1600s and a ruined future alike. No lace, ruffs, buckles or neat buttons; no zips, goggles or gadgets. Character prompts in `HANDOFF.md`.
- **How a video becomes frames** (scripts kept outside the repo, easy to redo): the owner makes a Veo video from a watercolour picture (white background; camera follows for walks, still for gestures), about 8 s. Check: camera steady (head x within a few px), height about 290 px, the planted foot slides back at a steady speed (`vpx`), stride width rises and falls evenly. Pick the loop whose first and last frames match best, cut a fixed window, white to transparent, grey `g = .2 + .62 * lightness`, crop, WebP q78.
- **Slow and atmospheric** (owner, build 21: "you can keep things slow, same with walk, I'm going for an atmospheric feel"): play every clip at its own video speed, never sped up. Christian walks at his video's pace: `MAXV` 1.05 (mud .6); the level takes about 1.5 minutes of walking. Neighbours hurry after him at 2.2, Obstinate walks home at 1.25 (his video's pace).
- **Faces stay plain** (like the original Christian): no drawn eyes or mouth, just a soft wash; add "no eyes, no mouth, no facial features, just a soft plain wash" to video prompts for faces seen from the front, especially for turns (Veo added eyes once). **But Christian's own side-on profile must show** (nose and chin inside the hood): "blank" does not mean an empty or black hood (owner, build 38).
- **Video tip:** for a turn or any move with a fixed ending, give Veo a start picture **and** an end picture (first and last frame); with only a start picture it turned Evangelist the wrong way.
- **Feet overlap the road a little** (owner: a gap under the feet looks wrong; overlapping reads right, like perspective): `FEET_SINK` (9 px) in `drawFrame`.
- **Don't cover the scene with words** (owner, twice): the dialogue is a thin strip of light text on the dark road under everyone's feet, no box.
- **Background hills should look realistic** (owner): natural ridgelines, not smooth waves or a single pointed peak.
- **Scale:** Christian must not dwarf things beside him (owner: "pilgrim dwarfs the fence"). Small things like fences go on a layer further back, sized for that distance.
- **The owner's chosen look** (lab default and the game's `LOOK`): `look=50.60.65.29.4.80.30.66.41.19.55.35.0-0.1.1.3` = Storm to gold, 4 layers, mist 29, soft edges 66, gentle hills.
- **Weather in the game runs across the whole level** (my choice, owner not yet asked): rain in the city, golden light by the gate.

## `story-lab.html` (Level 1 as a story walk, builds 25 to 40)

**Order of the level since build 50 (the book's order, `{10}` to `{28}`): OUT, THEN BACK.** He starts in the city in the rain, standing at his own house (door shut), with his book and burden → the child holds and he walks **left**, out of the street into the wheat field (the first two phrases of the tune are laid right to left: `back: true` in `PHRASES`) → Evangelist meets him in the field (Evangelist waits facing right, on video frame 178 held still, then points right, past Christian, to the light; Christian turns round to look: `P.turn = 1` in `lightTouched`), the child touches the light → he turns and runs **right**, back past his own door and on through the city. Owner, build 50: "i really like that out then back"; one house in one place, and it is the book's geography. Holding walks left until Evangelist has spoken (`G.evanDone`), right after. `?x=N` (testing) now always starts after Evangelist. → **he begins to run at once** (`G.runZone` is set when Evangelist finishes; "Hold to run.") → running, he comes to his own door, which is flung open: **the child chooses without being asked (build 49)**: keep holding and he runs straight past with his family crying after him, as in the book (`FAMILY_RUN`); let go by the house and he stops for the framed picture in the door's light (`FAMILY`), then the child holds to run on; either way he runs with his fingers in his ears crying "Life! The rule stays, but **the owner found build 40's levels too deep** (build 43: "too much disunity between his level and the next level back ... to see horizon like we do things have to have less perspective"): the picture is a flat side view with a far horizon, like a long lens, so levels near Christian differ from him only a little.
