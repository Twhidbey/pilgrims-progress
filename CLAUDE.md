# Pilgrim's Progress: notes for Claude

A gentle side-scrolling story walk for kids ages 7 to 10, following Bunyan's *The Pilgrim's Progress*. No build step, no dependencies. **Read `HANDOFF.md` ("START HERE") first in a new chat.** These notes were trimmed in build 140: the build-by-build history of Levels 1 and 2 was folded into the rules and code maps below; the full history is in git (`CLAUDE.md` at build 137).

## Files

- **`index.html` IS THE GAME** (owner, build 119: "let's make main link the real game"): the engine, the title page and Level 1 in the page; Level 2 in `slough.js` (object `SL`); Level 3 in `hill.js` (object `HL`). Pictures, frame packs and voices in `assets/`.
- `story-lab.html`: a few lines that send old links and Home Screen icons on to `index.html`; it keeps a build stamp so an old cached copy notices and reloads. Bump its stamp too.
- `book/pilgrims-progress-part-one.txt`: Bunyan's own text of Part One (public domain, no later editors), section numbers like `{18}`.
- `style-lab.html`: the art-style test page (the layered look; settled, no longer kept in step with the game). `concept-2200.html`: year-2200 concept pictures, waiting on the owner's choice (a quiet, gentle apocalypse; four directions on buttons, `#A3` = direction + version).
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `assets/icon-180.png`, `icon-512.png`: the Home Screen app (build 137, see Phones).
- `tools/`: `playtest.js`, `phone-touch-test.js`, `phone-test-3.js` (tests); `make-voice.py` (voices); `video-frames.py`, `gesture-frames.py`, `cutout.py` (turning Google's videos and pictures into game frames).

## The book

- **STAY CLOSE TO THE STORY (owner, build 48: "can you put sticking close to story in the notes").** Bunyan's order of events and his words where a child can follow them. Before adding, moving or rewording a story moment, read it in the book file and keep to it; where the game departs, tell the owner and list it below.
- **CHECK EVERY LEVEL AGAINST THE BOOK (owner, build 106: "please check everything with book to make sure it lines up (can you put this comment in notes for future level building)").** Before building a level and again before calling it done: read its whole stretch of the book, then go through the level line by line and moment by moment: (1) every line is the book's sentence, shortened and made plain, said by the person who says it in the book; (2) the moments come in the book's order; (3) nothing is shown that the book contradicts. List every departure here and tell the owner.
- **The words are Bunyan's, made plain (owner, build 56: "bring the words closer to book").** His sentences, shortened, the oldest words made plain.
- **The storyteller only tells the story (owner, build 108: "narrator should only say what's relevant to story - little side messages can tell user what to do").** Anything that tells the child what to do is a side message: a `hint()` while walking, or a `TIP` line inside a talk (quiet italics, no name, never spoken or recorded).
- **How gently to tell the dark parts** (Apollyon, the Valley of the Shadow, Faithful's death, Giant Despair) needs the owner's ruling, level by level.

### Departures from the book

**Level 1** (`{10}` to `{28}`): the book's first scenes at home (`{11}`, `{12}`) are left out; the level opens with him already turned from his house, his wife in the door; two of Evangelist's lines cut to shorten the talk ("why do you stand still?" and its answer, `{16}`); at his door the child may choose "Stop and look back": he stops, his wife calls, he answers "I cannot stay. I must find the way, for all of us" (ours; in the book he never stops; "Run on" is the book's way); "Go home" at Obstinate's question, and its words (`HOME`), are ours (nothing is lost); Obstinate and Pliable each stand in a lit doorway of their own as he runs by (the book only says they were neighbours); the city wall, the gate, "At the edge of the city stood a great gate" and the end line are ours; the talk with Pliable on the plain (`{25}` to `{28}`) was moved to open Level 2 (owner, build 68).

**Level 2** (`{25}` to `{36}`): "Many fears and doubts came over him, and settled in that place" is the storyteller's, while he wades (in the book Help explains it to the dreamer afterwards, `{33}`); Christian's first opening line puts "no more crying, nor sorrow" before the shining garments (the other way round in `{27}`); "in the mist" the steps were hard to see is ours (the book: "against change of weather", `{35}`); the choice to go back with Pliable is ours; he sinks deeper and deeper as he crosses (owner, build 114; the book only says he "began to sink"); Pliable's angry speech shortened; Christian's answer to Help shortened ("that I might escape the wrath to come" dropped); "Go on your way" spoken by Help (the book only reports that he bid him); the steps' sentence (`{35}`, said to the dreamer) given to the storyteller; the lights settling on the steps are our picture; Help turns and walks away after bidding him go (owner's choice, build 120; in the book he stays and speaks to the dreamer, `{33}` to `{36}`).

## Art direction

**The look (owner, Nov 14 2026):** scenery drawn in code; **atmospheric layered silhouettes, not video-game looking**: each depth layer basically one colour, overlapping with haze, **pale far layers, darker near layers**; soft rain on the left clearing to warm golden light on the right; simple, natural, calm, **not cartoonish**. The chosen look: `look=50.60.65.29.4.80.30.66.41.19.55.35.0-0.1.1.3` (`LOOK` in the game). Background hills realistic (natural ridgelines). The weather runs across the whole level (rain in the city, gold at the end); **the end is not fully golden** (owner, build 48: "still a tad dreary, though i do want progression"): `GOLD_MAX` caps it per level; raise it level by level.

**ART DIRECTION FOR EVERY LEVEL (owner, build 135): beauty first; each level one stunning picture.** Owner: "Really want to stress the artistic beauty and stunning nature of these levels ... The burning hill can be frightening - but most importantly stunning - it should awe first". **Before building a level, choose its ONE great picture and the master it learns from, and say so in its notes; a frightening thing must awe before it frightens.** Keep the game's own rules (layers of one tone, a flat side view, few things, a far light). Proposed masters: **John Martin** (tiny people under vast rock and fire: the hill, Apollyon, the Valley of the Shadow); **Caspar David Friedrich** (a lone figure from behind before something vast: the Cross, Hill Difficulty, the Delectable Mountains); **Rembrandt** (one warm light in a great dark: the lit doorways, the wicket-gate, the Interpreter's House, Palace Beautiful); **Turner** (light dissolving into gold and mist: the far light, Beulah, the River, the City); **Ansel Adams** (silver tones, one great tree: Level 2); **Van Gogh** (wheat under a moving sky: Level 1's field, if wanted); **Japanese woodblock landscapes** (Hasui, Yoshida: the layered look, Vanity Fair at night); games: *Journey* (a far light as the one goal), *Inside*/*Limbo* (depth by fog and silhouette), *GRIS* (colour returns as the story heals). **AGREED for Level 3:** John Martin for the hill, Rembrandt for Evangelist raising Christian and for the wicket-gate; Friedrich kept for the Cross, Turner for the end of the game.

**People:** monochrome watercolour figures rotoscoped from AI videos ("that walk looks AMAZING, more like this for other characters"). **AI image tools cannot make walk cycles; AI video can.** Frames are grey (`g = .2 + .62 * lightness`) and tinted in the game for the weather (`drawFrame`). **Clothes timeless** (a post-apocalyptic version may come): worn, handmade, layered cloth; no lace, ruffs, buckles, neat buttons, zips, goggles or gadgets. **Faces plain** (no eyes or mouth, a soft wash; say so in every video prompt), **but Christian's side-on profile shows** (nose and chin in the hood; neither black nor blank, owner build 38). **Slow and atmospheric** (owner, build 21): every clip at its own video speed, never sped up. **Feet overlap the ground a little** (a gap looks wrong; overlap reads like perspective), and **people stand and walk only on level ground or a very gentle rise** (put a bank's height into a short face).

**Picture rules (each from the owner):**
- **Flat, not deep** (builds 40 to 43): a flat side view with a far horizon, like a long lens. Things near Christian sit on depth levels with one rule (`level(p)`: size = p, sliding speed = p, standing line = `HORIZON + 140 * p`); levels near him differ only a little. **Light on the ground is flat:** a low wide pool; never a shape streaming down the picture toward us.
- **Stripped down and elegant** (build 44: "if you have a few things it begs for more"): do not add props; when in doubt take one away. **One special thing in the picture at a time** (build 40). Road things are flat side views (no tops or side faces). Small things go on a further layer, sized for it (Christian must not dwarf them).
- **Three levels of detail** (build 36): people the full watercolour; his house about two thirds of its painting; road things a filled outline with a faint trace (`HOUSE_DETAIL`, `PROP_DETAIL`).
- **Don't cover the scene with words** (twice): the words are a thin strip of light text on the dark road under everyone's feet, no box.
- Sizes on one level must agree (the doorway 146 road units high; people in doorways at `DOOR_FIG` of the door). Trees are plain English kinds only (oak, ash, elm; "don't [do] exotic ones"), painted pictures, no two alike in one scene, softer with distance.
- **Mist goes behind or beside a figure standing in something dark, never between the two.** Anything that goes round a figure (ripples) is drawn in two halves, one behind him and one in front. To keep something out of a mirror, draw it after the mirror. **A surface is never half mirror:** a place is either mud (dull, mirrors nothing) or standing water (mirrors); "more reflection" means more water. Do not draw one thing sharp on top of mist that hides everything round it.
- **Look at close-up stills at twice the size before calling a scene done** (`deviceScaleFactor: 2`).

## Rules for play

- **No jumps, no obstacles, no score, no counter** (owner, Nov 14: "atmospheric story driven game ... like Riven and Myst ... though I don't want the wandering"). One way to go: walk, a story moment, walk. Hold to walk; a tap only touches.
- **Nothing moves on its own except the words** (owner, build 107). A finger walks him only if it went down after the last words ended (`G.calm`) and was held `HOLD` ticks; when words end, fingers and keys are forgotten. **Any level's start that resets the clock `G.t` must reset `G.calm`** (build 136: Level 2 could not be walked by touch). Still automatic, on purpose: Pliable following, Obstinate going home, Level 2's fall, walking back after "Go back with Pliable", Level 3's veil of mist to the gate.
- **Words stay with what they tell of** (build 107): while words are being said he cannot reach the next place where new words begin (stop points); give the road enough room that the stops hold him for a moment at most (build 110: "more space between things so timing works out"). Positions are fixed numbers on the road; nothing flexes.
- **No skipping by accident** (build 29): a line cannot be tapped away until its recording has finished; a line moves on by itself a moment after it is spoken (build 73), except lines that ask for an act.
- **One text size** (builds 94, 98, 107, 136): every line the same size, from the picture's height only (`TXT_K`, never below `TXT_MIN` 16, at most `TXT_MAX`); a long line lifts the strip's top instead of shrinking. Owner: "too large is bad but too small is hard to read".
- **The hymn is discovered, never given away** (build 31): Amazing Grace is laid along the road as lights, one note per light (`PHRASES`, `BEAT`); the title only plays single soft notes. **Lights are not collected** (build 85: "too video gamey?"): each sings its note and drifts up into the far light. In Level 2 the tune is minor, low and bowed, and the last phrase comes in the major on the steps; in Level 3 it bends flat on the wrong path.
- Each new level: add its button on the title page and a title picture.

## Lessons (bugs that happened)

- **Never put a `//` comment before other code on the same line** (builds 62, 73, 101: it swallowed the code after it; once every voice went silent, once the page would not start). Comments on their own line or at the true end of a line. **Run a syntax check** (`new Function(source)`) before every push.
- After touching sound, check that recordings really start: wrap `AudioBufferSourceNode.prototype.start` in the headless test and count calls with a buffer longer than half a second.
- Never draw from a canvas that changed this frame into another canvas; bake once and draw from the bakes; draw each layer only in its band; don't touch `imageSmoothingQuality` (build 6: 12 frames a second otherwise). Don't rely on `ctx.filter` (iPhone): paint small and stretch up.
- `VW` is a `let` (the camera draws back at Level 1's gate): never cache it for drawing; anything in `drawWorld` that fills the screen uses `TOPY`; every `setTransform` there carries `OYD`.
- Anything seen in the opening picture loads in the first batch, before "Open the book" (build 89).
- A walking video with a still camera must be checked for sliding feet (build 119: work the travel out again from the planted foot). Videos may zoom though told not to: measure and undo (build 59). A foot drawn too high can be fixed in the pictures for free (lengthen the leg, build 118); **measure both feet of any new standing picture and put the ground at the higher one**. Blank paper caught between figures can be cleaned out of a pack (build 79; `--holes` in `gesture-frames.py`).
- Test walking with real touches, not only keys (build 136).

## Phones

- **The page updates itself** (`freshCopy`): it fetches its newest copy with `cache: 'no-store'` and reloads once onto `?v=N` if the build is higher. Keep that reload inside the same page, or the Home Screen app shows a browser bar.
- **Home Screen app** (build 137): the manifest (`display` fullscreen, `start_url`/`scope` "./"), the icon and the iPhone tags. An icon keeps the settings it was made with: an old icon must be deleted and added again from the main link.
- **Voices with the ringer on silent** (`mediaSound`: `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'` and a silent looping `<audio>`); every touch restarts sound iOS has left suspended (build 100). Not yet confirmed on the phone.
- **Text size:** iPhone Safari enlarges text in blocks it judges to be reading text (worse held sideways). `text-size-adjust: 100%` on `html, body`, and `fitWords` measures the drawn words and brings the size down (`fitC`). **Held upright** (build 138, owner: "when phone is in portrait mode can you make text smaller by default so it fits") the picture is only about 220 px high, so the size is `TXT_K_UP` (.052) of it, between `TXT_MIN_UP` (11) and 16; sideways is unchanged. Not provable off a real iPhone: if a line is still too big, ask which line, whether the phone was sideways, and whether Display Zoom or Larger Text is on.

- **Key:** the owner's, saved in the `pilgrims-progress` cloud environment as `GEMINI_API_KEY` (never print it; never in the repo). That environment allows `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` and `twhidbey.github.io`.
- **Pictures:** `gemini-3-pro-image` via `generateContent` with `responseModalities: ['TEXT', 'IMAGE']`, reference pictures as `inlineData` parts before the text; **a game frame as the style reference matches the watercolour people closely.** Give proportions in door-widths and an aspect ratio (`generationConfig.imageConfig.aspectRatio`). For a back view say "NOT a rucksack: no pockets, no flaps, no buckles, no bedroll". `python3 tools/cutout.py picture.png HEIGHT assets/name.webp` cuts a white-background figure and prints its META.
- **Video:** `gemini-omni-1.2-flash` through the Interactions API (`POST /v1beta/interactions`, `{model, input: [{type: 'image', data, mime_type}, {type: 'text', text}], response_modalities: ['video']}`; MP4 as base64 in `steps[].content[].data`), about 8 s. **Give a first and a last picture** (two image parts; the text says which is which) for any move with a fixed ending. Put the figure small in a 1280 x 720 white picture. Two people in one video works if the scene is drawn as flat blocks. Then: `tools/video-frames.py video.mp4 out.pack --height H --match assets/christian-walk.pack` (walks: picks the loop, prints the META) or `tools/gesture-frames.py` (one-off movements; takes a video or a folder of frames; `--frames a-b:step`). Check a walk: camera steady, planted foot slides back at a steady speed (`vpx`), stride rises and falls evenly.
- **Voices:** `GEMINI_API_KEY=... python3 tools/make-voice.py` reads every `[N/C/E/O/PL/W/H, '...']` line from `index.html` and `slough.js`, records only missing ones with Gemini TTS in the designed voices (descriptions in `HANDOFF.md`, IDs in the tool), checks each by transcription, deletes recordings of lines that no longer exist. **Change a line's words and you must run it again**, or it goes silent. `--redo C|N|...|all|styled`. **Direct the speech model with tags in square brackets** (`STYLE` in the tool: "[alarmed, breathless] Ah, neighbor ..."); a direction written as a sentence is read aloud. A voice is designed with `POST /v1beta/voices`, `store: true`. Bump `VOICE_V` when recordings change. **It does not read `hill.js` yet** (add the file, `WW` to `VOICES`/`WHO`/its pattern when the owner agrees to Level 3's voices).

## The engine and Level 1, City of Destruction (`index.html`)

**How Level 1 goes (the book's order, `{10}` to `{24}`; "out, then back", owner build 50: "i really like that out then back"):** the opening picture (`{10}`): rain, he stands turned from his own house, his wife in the lit door; he reads, weeps and trembles (`WEEP`) → hold to walk **left**, out into the wheat field (the first phrases laid right to left) → Evangelist waits in the field, breathing; the talk; the roll drifts into the tray; "Do you see that shining light?": he turns round (his turn video) and only then can the child touch the light → "the man began to run" (`RUNS`), then the child can run → running back **right** past his own house: the door flies open, his wife cries "Come back! Come home to us!", the child chooses "Stop and look back" (the framed picture in the lit door, his back to us, his answer, her second call, hold to run on) or "Run on"; then fingers in his ears ("Life! Life! Eternal life!", the world muffled) → the neighbours in their lit doorways mock him (three video loops) → Obstinate and Pliable in their own doorways; he tires to a walk (`RUN_END`); they overtake him; the choice "Go home" (a few dark steps, he turns back by himself) or "Go on to the light" → Obstinate goes home, Pliable comes → the city wall and gate: the camera draws far back (`ZOOM_GATE`), the child holds the gate to push it open, the tune plays whole, they walk through → end card, "Go on" to Level 2.

**Settled picture of Level 1:** a pale wheat field ("think simplicity and elegant effects": three layers, wind as bands of light, the crop thins into the mist at the field's end); a low 16th-century town (one or two floors, few windows, varied towers and spires); his cottage in a row of painted cottages (only lit doors open: his, the mockers', Obstinate's, Pliable's), each house with its own window style (`WIN_STYLES`, owner build 98); the wide glow at his house comes from its lit windows on the wet road; doorway shadows follow one light at the door's foot; nothing on the road but two tall lamps (lamplight pools radius 300); rain rings where he steps; a realistic city gate (about 10 m tower, 4 m wall, worn coping; the gate is a city gate, not a door), the far light rising above it; far away beyond the town, Level 2's oak in a lowland mist (`drawFarSlough`, using `SL.oakPic`: keep that export).

**Settled small points (each the owner's):** Evangelist faces Christian while they talk, only breathes while waiting (no hand movement), and never disappears (he stays pointing); Christian holds still in a talk until he is in his quiet breathing, then breathes on; at the opening only his wife stands in the door, her hand on the frame; the whole family when he runs back; going into the run there is no thick double picture (`poseChange(n, quick)`); no foreground grass and no leafy boughs (pixelated, blocky); no lit glow in the wheat; Pliable's voice is boyish.
